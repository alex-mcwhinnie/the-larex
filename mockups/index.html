The Larex — mockups

Four different directions, all using the current copy. Open each one and look for what you like.

  1. 01SwissLight paper, visible 12-column grid, huge tight grotesk with one red accent. Editorial and confident.
  2. 02TerminalAll monospace, typed-out headline, blinking cursor, keyboard shortcuts. Leans into the "development" side.
  3. 03AtelierWarm dark brown, large centred serif that blurs into focus, gold accents. Quiet and high-end.
  4. 04KineticElectric blue, giant scrolling marquees, lime button, cursor blob. Loud and playful.
