Unabridged

The reading tracker built for audiobooks first. Log what you're listening to, watch the hours add up, and keep your print books alongside, on your phone or in any browser.

Web · Available now iPhone · Coming soon
Open unabridged.app ↗

Features

Made for listeners. Ready for readers.

  • Audiobooks come first

    Most book trackers treat audiobooks as an afterthought. Unabridged puts the narrator, runtime, and listening hours at the centre of every book.

  • Search and autofill

    Find any audiobook by title or author. Narrator, exact runtime, series, and book number are filled in for you, along with the cover art.

  • Status, ratings, notes

    Queued, Active, Finished, or Abandoned, with a rating and private notes on every book. Finish one and the date is recorded for you.

  • Listening stats

    See hours listened, books finished over time, and your top authors, narrators, and genres.

  • Print books too

    Reading on paper? Print books get page counts and sit in the same library, so pages and listening hours are tracked side by side.

  • Bring your library

    Import an existing library from a CSV file and export yours whenever you like.

  • Synced everywhere

    Sign in with Apple or Google and your library follows you between browser and phone.

  • Private by design

    No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking. Delete your account and data at any time.

App details

The details.

Unabridged is free to use for libraries of up to 50 books. An optional Pro subscription, monthly or annual, removes the limit.
