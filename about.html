About

An independent studio making software people keep using.

The Larex LLC is a product design and development studio. We make our own consumer apps for the web and iPhone, and we handle every part of them ourselves: research, design, engineering, release, and support.

Founder

Who's behind it.

The Larex was founded by a product designer and developer who has spent years shaping digital products end to end, from early sketches through to shipped code.

The studio started with a simple frustration: there wasn't a good way to keep track of audiobooks and print books together. Unabridged was built to fix that.

What we believe

Build less. Build it well.

Respect people's data

Our apps don't show ads or sell data, and they collect only what's needed to work.

Design for every screen

Phone, tablet, and desktop each get a layout made for them, not a stretched copy.

Stand behind what we ship

Support requests come straight to the people who built the app.
